01 · DATA ANALYSIS

Customer Campaign Effectiveness Analysis

SQL · Excel
Business problem

Which customers show stronger campaign response patterns, and where could marketing effort be prioritised?

Dataset

2,205 customer records with purchasing behaviour, customer characteristics and campaign-response variables.

2,205customers
15.1%overall response rate
924avg spend, responders
30.3%response, top spend quartile
Campaign acceptance rate
Responders look more commercially engagedAverage total spend among responders is $924, compared with $498 among non-responders.
Response is not evenly distributedThe top quartile of customers by total spend has a 30.3% response rate, compared with 10.0% for the rest.
Campaign history mattersResponders accepted about 0.99 previous campaigns on average, versus 0.18 for non-responders.
Responder vs non-responder profile
Business implications

Use customer value and prior campaign engagement together rather than treating the full customer base as equally likely to respond.

Prioritise high-value, engaged customers for targeted testing while continuing to test whether lower-engagement groups can be activated cost-effectively.

This is an analytical interpretation of the dataset, not a claim about a real company's marketing strategy.

02 · BUSINESS ANALYSIS

SaaS Customer Support Process Improvement

Miro · Requirements · Process Mapping
Case study scenario

A B2B SaaS product receives technical support issues from customers. Some issues need specialist or engineering escalation. The case study explores how a clearer escalation process could improve handoffs, ownership and visibility.

Important boundary

This is a personal BA case study. It does not represent an internal ezyVet process and does not use confidential employer information.

Stakeholder mapAs-is processRoot cause RequirementsUser storiesAcceptance criteria
Stakeholders

Customer · reports the issue and needs clear communication

Support · investigates, documents and coordinates the case

Specialist / Engineering · investigates complex technical issues

Product · identifies recurring product or process opportunities

Team Lead · needs visibility of workload, ownership and escalations

Proposed future-state workflow
01 · ReceiveCapture issue and customer impact.
02 · TriageAssess severity, product area and urgency.
03 · TroubleshootComplete standard investigation.
04 · EscalateValidate required information before handoff.
05 · InvestigateSpecialist team investigates and records progress.
06 · UpdateKeep ownership and customer communication visible.
07 · ResolveConfirm the solution and customer outcome.
08 · CloseDocument the resolution and learning.
Example requirement
FR01
Required troubleshooting information should be captured before a technical case can be escalated.
Example user story

As a support associate, I want required troubleshooting details captured before escalation, so that the specialist team receives enough information to begin investigation.

Acceptance criteria

• Mandatory fields are visible before escalation.

• A case cannot be escalated when required information is missing.

• The receiving team can view the complete escalation record.

• Ownership and status are visible to authorised users.

03 · BUSINESS REPORTING

New Zealand Employment Reporting Dashboard

Excel · Tableau
Business problem

How can employment data be turned into a concise reporting view that helps a business understand current labour-market movement and decide what needs further investigation?

Source

Stats NZ Business Employment Data and Employment Indicators. The latest available data at the time of building this portfolio includes August 2026 employment indicators.

2.36mfilled jobs, Aug 2026
+0.2%month-on-month
$16.6bgross earnings, Aug 2026
+3.8%annual earnings growth
Dashboard questions

1. Is employment moving up or down?

2. Which industries or regions are changing most?

3. Are employment and earnings moving in the same direction?

4. Which movements should a business leader investigate further?

Design

KPI cards → time trend → industry comparison → regional comparison → written interpretation and limitations.

What the dashboard demonstrates

Rather than presenting charts for their own sake, the reporting layer is designed around a business question and an action: understand the movement, identify where it is concentrated, then decide what requires deeper analysis.
